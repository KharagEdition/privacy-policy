Privacy Policy

Tibetan Dictionary for Android. Effective October 21, 2026.

In short

  • The dictionary works entirely on your device. There are no accounts.
  • Your searches, search history and saved words never leave your phone.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs and improve the app.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Tibetan Dictionary (“the app”) is published by Kharag Edition (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. If you have questions, write to tashi@kharagedition.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

All dictionary entries are bundled with the app and searched on your device. The following is stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone:

  • words you search for and your search history
  • saved words and favourites
  • your app settings

We never receive this information. You can clear your history or remove saved words in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it permanently.

3. Usage statistics and crash reports

To understand how the app is used and to fix problems, the app uses two services provided by Google: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They automatically collect:

ServiceWhat is collected
Firebase Analytics A random app-instance ID, general usage events (such as app opens, screens viewed and session length), app version, Android version, device model, language setting, and approximate country or region derived from your IP address.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error details (such as the code location of the crash), app version, Android version, device model, free memory and storage, and a random installation ID.

This data does not include your name, email address, the words you search for, your search history or your saved words. We use it only to find and fix bugs and to see which features are used, never to identify you or to show you ads.

These services are always active while you use the app. If you do not want this data collected, you can stop it by uninstalling the app.

Google processes this data on our behalf. You can read more in Firebase’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: used only to send usage statistics and crash reports. Searching and every dictionary feature work fully offline.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Usage statistics and crash reports are shared only with Google, which processes them for us as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law.

When you use “Share” on an entry, the content goes to the app you choose, and that app’s own privacy policy applies.

6. Advertising and purchases

The app shows no ads, offers no in-app purchases and does not use your device’s advertising ID.

7. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s data or uninstall.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.
  • Crashlytics crash reports are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically.

8. International transfers

Google may process usage statistics and crash reports on servers in the United States and other countries. Google applies safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

9. Security

Usage statistics and crash reports are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). Data stored on your device is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not collect names or contact details, we may need your help to identify the data that relates to you. To make a request, email tashi@kharagedition.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and improving it to process usage statistics and crash reports.

11. Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages and requires no account. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we hold information about a child, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app.
